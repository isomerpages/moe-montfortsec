<h3>Mission</h3>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>strengthen partnership and engagement with parents to improve children’s
well-being and learning.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>provide parents with practical information/ parenting skills to support
their child’s learning</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>render active support of school programmes.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>facilitate effective 2 way communication between school and parents.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>foster better parent/child bonding.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
